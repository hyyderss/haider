What We Do

Four Stages. Zero Compromise.

Most commercial salt is blasted with explosives, bleached, then cut with anti-caking agents. We do not. Every crystal that leaves Khewra passes through four stages, and not one of them touches a chemical.

01

Ethical Extraction

Hand-mined by skilled workers at fair wages, no explosives, no mechanized blasting.

02

The Purification Wash

Rinsed in natural spring water to clear surface sediment before any processing begins.

03

Precision Crafting

Ground to exact specification, from micro-fine powder to coarse culinary crystals.

04

The Purity Seal

Optical color sorting, metal detection, then a hermetic seal before it ever leaves the facility.
